Pasta Recipe

Home

Pasta is a quick, delicious, and universally loved dish that originates from Italy.

It is made by boiling durum wheat noodles and tossing them in a flavorful sauce topped with fresh herbs.

It is the perfect meal for busy weekdays because it is both satisfying and incredibly easy to prepare.

Ingredients

  • 200g spaghetti noodles
  • 2 tablespoons of olive oil
  • 3 cloves of minced garlic
  • 1 can of crushed tomatoes
  • 1/2 cup of grated parmesan cheese
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • Salt and red pepper flakes

Steps

  1. Boil water in a large pot with a pinch of salt and cook the spaghetti until it is tender.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a pan over medium heat and sauté the minced garlic for one minute.
  3. Pour the crushed tomatoes into the pan, season with salt and red pepper flakes, and simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. Drain the cooked spaghetti noodles, leaving a small amount of pasta water aside.
  5. Toss the spaghetti directly into the tomato sauce and mix well, adding pasta water if it looks too dry.
  6. Serve the hot pasta on a plate topped with grated parmesan cheese and fresh basil leaves.
