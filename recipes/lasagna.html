Lasagna Recipe

Home

Lasagna is a world-famous comfort food originally from Italy.

It is made by stacking wide sheets of pasta with various fillings like chopped meats, local vegetables, and aromatic spices.

The whole dish is topped with a heavy layer of cheese and baked until the edges get completely crisp and brown.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna pasta sheets
  • 500g ground beef
  • 1 jar of marinara sauce
  • 2 cups of ricotta cheese
  • 3 cpus of shredded mozarella cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 1 teaspoon of dried oregano
  • salt and black pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Cook the ground beef in a large skillet over medium heat until browned, then drain the excess fat.
  2. Pour the marinara sauce into the skillet with the beef and let it simmer for 5 minutes.
  3. In a medium bowl, mix the ricotta cheese, egg, dried oregano, salt, and pepper together.
  4. Boil the lasagna sheets in salted water according to the package instructions, then drain them.
  5. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce at the bottom of your baking dish.
  6. Arrange a layer of pasta sheets over the sauce, followed by a layer of the ricotta mixture and mozzarella cheese.
  7. Repeat the layers of sauce, pasta, and cheeses until all your ingredients are used up.
  8. Cover the top with a heavy layer of mozzarella cheese and bake at 190°C (375°F) for 25 minutes until bubbling.
