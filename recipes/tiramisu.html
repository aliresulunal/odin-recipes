Tiramisu Recipe

Home

Tiramisu is a famous coffee-flavored Italian dessert made of ladyfingers dipped in coffee, layered with a whipped mixture of eggs, sugar, and mascarpone cheese, and flavored with cocoa.

It is a rich, creamy, and sophisticated treat that is served cold.

The name literally means "pick me up" due to its shot of espresso.

Ingredients

  • 24 ladyfinger biscuits
  • 1 cup of strong brewed espresso (cooled)
  • 250g mascarpone cheese
  • 2 eggs (separated into yolks and whites)
  • 1/2 cup of granulated sugar
  • 1 tablespoon of cocoa powder
  • 1 teaspoon of vanilla extract

Steps

  1. Whisk the egg yolks and sugar together in a medium bowl until light and creamy.
  2. Add the mascarpone cheese and vanilla extract to the yolk mixture, mixing smoothly until no lumps remain.
  3. In a separate clean bowl, beat the egg whites until stiff peaks form, then gently fold them into the mascarpone cream.
  4. Dip each ladyfinger biscuit into the cooled espresso quickly so they do not get too soggy.
  5. Arrange a layer of the dipped ladyfingers tightly at the bottom of a small rectangular serving dish.
  6. Spread half of the mascarpone cream mixture evenly over the ladyfinger layer.
  7. Repeat with another layer of dipped ladyfingers and top it with the remaining cream mixture.
  8. Dust the top generously with cocoa powder, cover the dish, and refrigerate for at least 4 hours before serving.
